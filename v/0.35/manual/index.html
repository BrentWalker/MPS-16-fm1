MPS-16

SEED, MPS-16 ver FM1

SEED is the chord builder of MPS-16, as firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1. Play a few notes you like, pick a scale that fits them, and the 16 white keys hold that key's chords and progressions. The black keys play the notes of the key and light up the bass of every chord you play. It sends everything out over USB MIDI, so it can drive the synths you already have.

This is an early test build by BeatSmith.

1Install

From Chrome or Edge on a computer, with the FM-1 connected by USB: open the installer and follow it. The installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back. You can also try SEED in the browser first: it runs the same code, with sound.

2The first start: COACH

The first time SEED starts, it asks whether to show its steps. Turn SELECT to choose NO, NOT NOW, THIS FIRST TIME or EVERY START, then press PLAY. You can change it later on the SYS page (K4).

COACH is six short steps. Each one says what to do, and the keys and buttons it means light up or blink. When you do the step, it moves on by itself:

  1. PICK YOUR NOTES: play a few notes you like, on any keys.
  2. PICK A SCALE: turn SELECT to a scale that fits, press PLAY.
  3. PLAY THE PADS: the white keys blink. Play a few.
  4. PLAY THE BASS: the bright black key is the bass of the chord you just played.
  5. SHAPE IT: turn K2 for 7ths and 9ths, K3 for how the pads play.
  6. BASS OR NOT: try one of the last four black keys.

HOME skips the steps. Hold SYS to start them again any time.

3The keys: A, B and C pads

  • A pads: the 16 white keys. Pads 1 to 8 are your key's chords (1 to 7, then the first one an octave up). Pads 9 to 16 are progressions, four bars each. One progression plays at a time: start another and the first stops.
  • B pads: the first 7 black keys. The notes of your key, from the root up.
  • C pads: the last 4 black keys. BASS, NO BASS, NO ROOT and TAP.

The B pads' lights show the chord you played last, so you always know what to play along:

LightMeans
Brightthe bass of the chord
Blinkingthe bass, taken out by NO BASS or NO ROOT: still the note to play under it
Midthe rest of the chord
Dimthe rest of the key

The screen shows the same thing in the Earth Science colours, as MPS-16 ver WEB does: brown the bass, red the root, blue the chord, an outline for a note that was taken out.

BASS, NO BASS, NO ROOT: BASS plays the chords as they are, and the progressions with a bass line. NO BASS drops each chord's bottom note and the bass line. NO ROOT drops the root too, as long as two notes stay.

TAP: tap it on the beat a few times and the BPM follows. Its light flashes on the beat.

A chord pad plays while you hold it. A progression plays once when you tap it, loops while you hold it past the end, and stops when you let go after it looped. SEQ is LATCH: with it on, pads keep playing after you let go, and tapping a playing pad stops it.

5PICK NOTES

Press EDIT. Every key is now a note, like a normal keyboard. Play the notes you like: each one you play is added, and playing it again takes it out. The scales that hold all of them are listed below, best first (the first note you played as the root). Turn SELECT to choose one and press PLAY: the pads are rebuilt in that key. K1 clears your notes. EDIT again (or HOME) goes back without changing anything.

6LIB

Press SEL. K1 picks what's listed: TRIADS, 7THS, 9THS, SUS2, SUS4, ADD9 or PROGRESSIONS. Turn SELECT to choose one, PLAY to hear it, and a white key puts it on that pad. It follows the key when you change it.

7PAD EDIT and LOCK

ENV opens PAD EDIT for the pad you played last, as the web's pad edit window. The top shows the pad's notes and a playhead crosses them while it plays: a progression's four bars, or one bar of how a chord plays (CHORD, STRUM, ARP or STABS). Brown is the lowest note, white the notes sounding now. Play any pad and the screen follows it.

PLAY page:

K3INVERSION, -3 to +3

ENV again: the MIDI FX page, the web's MIDI FX strip:

ControlDoes
K1STRUM: the chord's notes one after another, OFF to 200 ms (STRUM plays: 35 ms when OFF)
K2HUMANIZE: each note a little off in time and velocity, like a player
K3SWING: the off-beats late, STRAIGHT to 75%
K4ARP: UP, DOWN, UP-DOWN, RANDOM (for ARP plays)
SELECTVELOCITY: as you play it, or a fixed one

ENV a third time goes home. Hold HOME while you turn a knob and it sets every pad at once (the web's ALL PADS).

LFO is LOCK: it locks the pad you played last, so it stays exactly as it is when the key, type or bass mode changes. LFO again unlocks it. A small corner marks a locked pad.

8REC, UNDO, SAVE and LOAD

REC: the A pads blink. Tap the pad to record into, then a bar of clicks counts you in. Play the B pads, the chord pads or MIDI in, and press REC again to stop. The take is rounded to whole bars and goes on that pad. REC before the count ends cancels.

ARP is UNDO: one step back (a LIB drop, a take, clearing the pads, a LOAD). ARP again goes forward.

SHIFT + PLAY (hold HOME, press PLAY) clears every pad that isn't locked. Turn PRESET to bring the kit back, or ARP to undo.

SAVE keeps the pads, their PAD EDIT and the settings. Hold SAVE to LOAD them back.

9SOUND, MIDI and SETUP

FX opens SOUND. Each of the four groups plays its own FM-1 sound: K1 pads 1 to 8, K2 pads 9 to 16, K3 the B pads, K4 the keyboard. Eight sounds: WARM, KEYS, ORGAN, STRINGS, PLUCK, BASS, BELL, LEAD. SELECT sets OUT:

  • SOUND AND MIDI: the FM-1 plays and MIDI goes out
  • SOUND ONLY: no MIDI out
  • MIDI ONLY: the FM-1 stays quiet and drives your synths (REC's count-in click still sounds)

MIDI coming in plays the sound of its channel (1 to 4), and channels above 4 play the keyboard's.

MIDI goes out over USB and Bluetooth (the FM-1's TRS jack is MIDI in only), on four fixed channels:

ChannelWhat
1Pads 1 to 8 (chords)
2Pads 9 to 16 (progressions)
3B pads
4The keyboard: PICK NOTES and LIB previews

Set your synths to listen on those. SETUP shows the map too. PLAY sends all notes off on every channel.

SYS (tap) opens SETUP: K1 Bluetooth MIDI on or off, K2 MIDI clock out (24 a beat, at the BPM), K3 MIDI clock in (the BPM follows it while it comes), K4 the steps (OFF, FIRST, ALWAYS). Hold SYS for the steps.
